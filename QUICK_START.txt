BSS BOOSTER — EASY START

1. Launch Bss Booster.
2. Click HOTBAR. Under Item usage, choose Gumdrops or another item for
   the same slot number you have assigned in Roblox.
3. Set Every = how often to press that number key (e.g. 2 sec).
4. Tick Use OR click the red AUTO: OFF button on the game overlay.
5. Right-drag a red button to line up all 7 buttons under Roblox's bar.
6. Press F1, then click back into Roblox. F2 = pause; F3 = stop.

Want an alarm?
1. Click ALARMS and + New alarm.
2. Enter its name, time, reset shortcut, and Sound or Voice.
3. Click Start for that alarm. Reset silences the noise and restarts it.

Show only hotbar, only alarms, both or neither?
In HOTBAR or SETTINGS, toggle the Show hotbar / Show alarms checkboxes.
All timers continue when hidden; alarm sounds and voice continue too.

Your items, settings and alarms save automatically when you close the app.
A new install starts completely blank.

Want a Windows EXE without installing Python? See BUILD_WITHOUT_PYTHON.md.

OVERLAY BOTTOM-EDGE FIX (v1.3.1)
Right-drag a red AUTO button down to the very bottom edge of your screen.
In Hotbar or Settings, you can also click "Move to screen bottom".
The button position will save when you close Bss Booster.
The Windows taskbar may overlap the buttons if it is visible.

V1.4.0: CHOOSE VISIBLE RED BUTTONS AND MOVE ALARMS SEPARATELY
HOTBAR > "Choose which AUTO buttons appear on-screen": tick Slot 1-7
for only the pills you want to SEE. Hiding a red pill does NOT stop its
macro, and hidden slot positions stay empty so the others remain aligned.
Click Show all 7 or Hide all 7 for convenience.

To move red buttons, right-drag one red AUTO pill.
To move alarm timers separately, right-drag an alarm countdown chip.
To center alarm timers again, click "Reset alarm overlay position" in ALARMS.
Both overlay locations and visibility choices save when you close the app.
Click-through mode is no longer present: buttons and alarms always support mouse dragging.

V1.4.2: CLICK-THROUGH REMOVED
You can always left-click an AUTO button or right-drag it to move the row.
Right-drag an alarm chip to move the alarm overlay independently.
Old saved click-through settings are automatically ignored; profiles are kept.

V1.4.3 NAVIGATION
Use the tabs along the TOP of the app (Hotbar, Timers, bell Alarms, Profiles, Status, Settings). The left sidebar has been removed. Add alarms from the Alarms page.
